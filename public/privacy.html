Privacy

Kuro has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. On the House Software keeps nothing about you, your family or your drawings.

Last updated November 26, 2026

Who we are

Kuro is a free figure-drawing practice app made by On the House Software (OTHS). It's meant for adults to set up and use with their kids. Questions about this page go to onthehousesoftware@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your drawings, their names and stars, and the figures Kuro makes.
  • Your settings, including any Gemini key, Cloudflare account ID and Cloudflare token you add.

All of this is saved in the browser's storage on this device only. We can't see it, and nothing is synced anywhere.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

Making a figure needs an AI service. Kuro only uses the one you connect yourself.

If you connect your own Cloudflare account

The pose, angle and amount of detail you pick, your account ID and your token are sent to Kuro's server, which asks your Cloudflare account to draw the figure and passes the picture back. Your drawings aren't sent. Your token goes through our server on its way to your own account. We never save or log it.

If you add your own Gemini key

Your Gemini key goes straight to Google from this device. It never passes through our server. Along with your pose choices, Kuro sends up to 3 of your starred drawings to Google so the figure can follow your drawing style.

Whatever you send to Cloudflare or Google is covered by their terms and privacy policies, because it's your account with them.

Things every website sees

  • Like any website, the server that delivers Kuro sees your device's internet address while it answers a request. It's used to answer that request. When you make a figure or test a connection, it's also counted for about a minute so one device can't flood the server. We don't keep it, and request logging is turned off.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ins, and no cookies.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share anything, because we don't keep anything to share.

Children

Kuro is built for a grown-up to set up and to use alongside a child. We don't ask for or collect personal information from anyone, children included. A grown-up should be the one who adds any key or account, and who decides which drawings get starred.

Backups and deleting everything

  • Save a backup file in the Library makes a file with your drawings and settings. Keys and tokens are never in a backup file. The file is yours, and it goes wherever you save it.
  • Remove all my keys in Settings takes every key and token off this device.
  • Clearing this site's data in the browser, or uninstalling Kuro, deletes everything Kuro saved on the device.

Check it yourself

Kuro's code is public, so anyone can check that it does what this page says.
