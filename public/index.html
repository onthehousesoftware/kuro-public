How Kuro works

Kuro makes new figures to practice drawing, and keeps your drawings in a library on this device.

Practice

  • Pose: pick one, like Running or Sitting, or type your own idea in "Or describe one." Surprise me lets Kuro choose.
  • View: which side you see the person from: front, three-quarter, side, back, from above or from below.
  • Detail: Quick gesture is loose and fast. Clean figure is a simple sketch. Fully finished adds clothes details and shading.
  • Make a figure: draws a new figure. It takes about 10 to 30 seconds.
  • Pose timer: pick a time and tap Start, then draw the figure before the clock runs out. It chimes when time's up. Pause and Reset do what they say.
  • Save to library: keeps a figure you like so you can practice it again later.

Library

  • Take a photo of a drawing: opens the camera. Lay the page flat in good light and fill the screen with it.
  • Add from photos or a scan: picks pictures already on this device. You can choose several at once.
  • Clean up like a scan: makes the paper whiter and the lines darker. Turn it off if a drawing looks too harsh.
  • Use as a style example: stars a drawing (★). With a Gemini key, Kuro shows up to 3 starred drawings to Gemini so new figures look more like yours.
  • Tap any drawing to rename it, star it, save the image to this device, practice it again or delete it. Delete asks twice so nothing goes by accident.
  • Filters: All, My drawings, or Kuro figures (the ones Kuro made).

Settings

  • Your Cloudflare account (free): an adult makes a free Cloudflare account and a token that can only use Workers AI, then pastes the token and account ID here. Figures come from that account's free daily allowance, about 100 a day. Step-by-step guide.
  • Gemini API key (optional, paid): Google's Gemini can actually look at your starred drawings, so figures come out closest to your own style. It costs about 4¢ a figure from prepaid credit. Step-by-step guide.
  • Gemini credit balance: Kuro's own estimate, because Google doesn't let apps read the real balance. AI Studio's Billing page has the exact number.
  • When the Gemini credits run out, Kuro switches to the Cloudflare account by itself if one is connected. When you add more credit, type the new amount and tap Set balance.
  • Remove all my keys takes every key and token off this device.

Good to know

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your drawings stay on this device. A drawing only leaves it when Gemini is shown your starred drawings to make a figure.
  • The free Cloudflare drawings run out each day. When they do, Kuro tells you the time they come back, in your own time zone.
  • No internet? Your library and the timer still work. Making figures needs the internet.
  • Clearing the browser's data or uninstalling Kuro deletes the library. Use Save a backup file in the Library now and then to keep them safe.
  • If a figure doesn't look right, just tap Make a figure again. Every one comes out different.

Where your keys go

Keys and tokens are saved only on this device. They're never in a backup file.

Your Gemini key goes straight from this device to Google. Your Cloudflare token goes through Kuro's server on its way to your own account. We never save or log it.
